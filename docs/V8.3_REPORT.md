# V8.3 Driving experience refinement

Scope: the driving camera, a few justified handling refinements, and wheel / lamp / body animation on the existing car.
No map data, roads, buildings, landmarks, rendering modes, shader precompilation or chunk scheduling were changed. No
new dependency, model or download.

Status: implemented and measured; **not committed, not pushed, not deployed**.

Machine for every number below: Intel UHD Graphics (ANGLE / Direct3D 11), Edge, medium preset, adaptive resolution
off, production builds served by `vite preview`. Baseline = V8.2 (`df08277`), built and measured in the same session,
alternating with the V8.3 build. Raw data: `docs/perf-v8.3.json`.

## 1. What was there already (inspected first)

`src/vehicle/` already had a separate chase camera (mouse orbit, wheel zoom, steer lead, wall rays, look-back while
reversing, speed FOV), wheel spin from travelled distance, front-wheel steer, and body squat / dive / roll on a group
that leaves the wheels and the collision body alone. So Task 3 items 1, 2 and 5 existed; brake and reversing lamps did
not. The camera and handling problems below were found by measurement, not assumed.

## 2. Camera (Task 1) - `src/vehicle/ChaseCamera.ts`

Problems found in V8.2:

| Problem | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Wall pull-in could leave the camera behind a wall for a few frames | The boom eased in at rate 24 and was clamped to `allowed + 0.6`, with a 0.3 m wall margin: up to 0.3 m past the wall surface |
| Boom length flickered at speed | Route D (2 km, ~95 km/h): 29-34 frame-to-frame boom length reversals per run, jumps up to 0.36 m |
| Camera too close to walls beside it | 0.3 m margin vs. a near plane that reaches ~0.4 m sideways at 74 deg FOV; look-around sweep in the lane: 4 frames with a wall within 0.2 m |
| Nothing kept the camera out of parked cars when the boom swings low over a parked row | only walls were tested (rays at roof height) |
| Lag of the follow springs depended on the frame rate | `x += (target - x) * damp(k, dt)` trails a moving target by a dt-dependent amount |
| Reversing for 0.9 s flipped the view 180 deg at up to ~5 rad/s | short shunts in the lane whipped the camera round |

Changes:

- **Frame-rate-exact follow** (`follow()`): the pivot spring and the yaw follow treat the target as moving steadily
  over the frame, so the camera trails the car by the same amount at 20, 60 or 144 fps (unit-tested, < 0.12 m apart).
- **Walls cut the boom at once and it eases back out**; zoom itself is eased separately, so wheel / V changes stay
  smooth both ways. Wall margin 0.3 -> 0.45 m, side rays as wide as the near plane, plus two short side rays that step
  the camera away from a frontage running beside it.
- **Low-obstacle lift**: if the camera point is inside a parked car, kiosk or other outline, it rises over it at once
  (0.45 / 0.9 / 1.35 / 1.8 m) and settles back down only when clear.
- **Reversing**: looks over the tail only after 1.5 s of reversing faster than 2.5 m/s, and every automatic swing is
  capped at 2.6 rad/s on top of the car's own turning (a pan, not a whip). Back to forward when the car drives forward.
- **Adjustable distance**: `V` at the wheel (previously unused while driving) cycles 4.8 / 6.6 / 9.2 m; the wheel
  still sets anything from 3.6 to 14 m. Controls line shows `V camera`.
- Unchanged: mouse orbit, recentre delay, steer lead, FOV with speed, pivot height, walking and OP fly cameras.

## 3. Handling (Task 2) - `src/vehicle/VehiclePhysics.ts`

Measured on a flat stub world (`node tools/dev/handling-probe.ts`), before -> after:

| Test | V8.2 | V8.3 |
|---|---|---|
| 0-50 / 0-80 km/h | 3.2 s / 6.7 s | unchanged |
| Brake 50-0 km/h | 8.7 m, 1.3 s | unchanged |
| Handbrake 50-0 km/h | 12.9 m | 11.9 m |
| **Coast 50-0 km/h (throttle released)** | **211 m, 33 s** | **91 m, 13.5 s** |
| Low-speed turning radius | 3.6 m | unchanged (Jalan China lane manoeuvres unchanged) |
| **Time to 90 % steering lock at 50 / 70 / 90 km/h** | **50 / 33 / 17 ms** | **283 / 333 / 367 ms** |
| Time to 90 % lock at 10 km/h | 233 ms | 233 ms |
| Reverse top speed | 22 km/h | unchanged |
| 11 % slope: parked creep / 0-30 km/h uphill | 0 m / 2.3 s | unchanged |
| Same 8 s scripted drive at 20 / 30 / 60 / 144 fps | identical to 2 cm | identical to 4 cm |

Two refinements, both justified by the numbers above:

1. **Steering rate in proportion to the lock available** (`steerEaseFast`). At speed the grip-limited lock is tiny,
   and the fixed 2.4 rad/s wheel rate reached it in one or two frames: a tap of A / D at 90 km/h jumped straight to
   the full 8.5 m/s^2 of lateral grip. Now full lock takes ~0.25 s when slow (as before) and up to ~0.4 s at speed.
2. **Lift-off engine braking** 0.6 m/s^2 (`engineBrake`): a released throttle rolled a car on for 200 m, i.e. past
   the next junction from any street in the old town. Coasting still keeps most of the speed for a few seconds.

Not changed (no measured need): acceleration, brakes, grip, slip, handbrake behaviour, slopes, collision response,
sub-stepping, safe R reset. Collision and reset code are untouched.

## 4. Animation and lamps (Task 3) - `src/vehicle/CarModel.ts`

- Wheel spin from distance (existing) verified: 15.23 m travelled -> 52.5 rad = travel / 0.29 m radius.
- Front-wheel steering (existing) verified: wheel angle = physics steer angle (0.424 rad in the steer test).
- **Brake lamps** (new): lit with the service brake or the handbrake. **Reversing lamp** (new): lower row of the
  tail cluster turns white while reversing (or S held from rest). Both are copies of the kit's own tail-lamp triangles
  lifted 4-7 mm off the body with an unlit material, shown only while lit: zero draw calls otherwise, +1 or +2 while
  lit. Off whenever nobody is at the wheel.
- The lamp program is compiled on getting in (`Driving.prepare` -> `prepareForDraw` / the V7 pipeline's `prepare`):
  the V8.3 probe showed 97 -> 98 programs on the *first brake* before this, now 98 on entry and no change on braking.
- Body pitch / roll (existing): smoothing made frame-rate independent (`1 - exp(-9 dt)` instead of `min(1, 9 dt)`).
  Measured: -0.036 rad nose-dive braking, +0.015 rad squat accelerating, 0.011 rad roll in the low-speed turn. It
  rotates only the body group: wheels, ground contact and the collision box are unaffected.

CPU per frame (ms): physics 0.12-0.68 both builds (unchanged); **camera 0.04-0.20 -> 0.08-0.32 (+0.04-0.13 ms**:
two side rays and the obstacle check). Render call (CPU submit + GPU wait on this integrated GPU; no separate GPU timer

already noted in V8.2. Worst single frames vary by +-20-40 ms between runs of the same build (cold shader / chunk
work), in both directions. On the medians and the second pair there is **no sustained frame-time regression**.

Low frame rate (4x CPU throttle, B and C): V8.2 16.0 / 19.1 fps, V8.3 17.3 / 20.1 fps; p95 134 / 115 vs 114 / 123 ms.
No regression; the camera is markedly steadier there (section 5).

## 7. Remaining limitations

- The obstacle lift and wall tests use the collision outlines: anything without one (lamp posts, signboards, awnings,
  trees) can still be passed through by the camera, as in V8.2.
- The five-foot-way arcades are passages in the collision world, so a side view can look out from under an arcade
  roof (seen mid-pan in `after-04-reverse-start`). Not a clip, but not a pretty angle.
- Brake-lamp red is shifted toward orange by the scene tone mapping; there is no glow or light cast (deliberately
  cheap).
- Body lean is an animation driven by the physics accelerations; there is no suspension simulation (not requested).
- The look-over-the-tail behaviour while reversing is kept from V8 (now delayed and slowed); if a GTA-style
  always-forward view is preferred, it is one constant (`reverseDelay`) to set very high.
- Performance pairs are 2 x normal + 1 x throttled; single-run variance on this machine is several ms at p95/p99.
