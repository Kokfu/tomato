### Modifications

Done with glTF-Transform (`@gltf-transform/core`, `functions`, `extensions`) as a one-off dev-time step; it is not a project dependency.

- Kept only the `Idle`, `Walk` and `Run` animations (21 other clips removed: weapons, punches, death, roll, strafes, wave...) and renamed them from `CharacterArmature|<name>` to `<name>`.
- `resample` (removes redundant keyframes), `prune`, `dedup`.
- `quantize` (position 14 bit, normal 10 bit) which adds the `KHR_mesh_quantization` extension. three.js' `GLTFLoader` supports it natively, so no Draco/meshopt decoder is shipped.
- Geometry, skeleton, skin weights and material colours are unchanged. The model has no textures (flat material colours).

At run time (`src/player/GlbCharacter.ts`) the model is scaled to 1.72 m, its materials are replaced by Lambert materials with the same colours, and the airborne pose is taken from the flight phase of the run cycle (the asset has no jump clip).

### Modifications (road set)

Resized with Pillow (Lanczos), roughness moved into the alpha channel of the albedo image, saved as lossy WebP. In the shader (`asphaltPhoto` in `src/v5/materials.ts`) the albedo is divided by its own mean colour and multiplied by the building's / road's existing vertex colour, so the photograph supplies surface texture, roughness and normals while the verified road colour is unchanged. Applied to the Jalan China carriageway only. The two files (493,712 bytes) are fetched only when the Jalan China section is built with real assets on; the Low preset skips the normal map.

## V7.4 pedestrians (Jalan China section)

No new asset, and the prototype is **disabled by default** (see docs/V7.4_REPORT.md): while it is off, nothing is loaded for it. When switched on for development (`?peds=on`), the two or three pedestrians in the Jalan China section (`src/v5/pedestrians.ts`) are clones of the player's "Casual Character" above (Quaternius, CC0), re-coloured at run time (shirt, trousers, shoes, hair, skin tone) and scaled to 1.58 - 1.72 m. They use the same `Idle` and `Walk` clips. The model is fetched once more when the section is first shown (browser cache) and freed when it is hidden.

Considered and not added in this batch: the other Quaternius CC0 characters on the same rig ("Business Man", "Hoodie Character") would give different silhouettes for about 400 kB each; realistic scanned humans with a CC0 or CC BY licence and a walk cycle were not found in a form small enough for this project.

No audio file was added either: a licensed street recording of suitable quality was not sourced. The synthesised ambience from V5 is unchanged (it starts only after the Start click and follows the existing sound switch).
