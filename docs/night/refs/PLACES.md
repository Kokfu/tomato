# M5 hero places: references and gaps

All URLs are Wikimedia Commons `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<name>` (replace spaces with `_`).
Only CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or public-domain files are listed. Local copies (640 px) are in `img/` where marked [img].
No Google imagery was used. Camera positions are approximate, derived from the photo content.

**Coordinate warning.** `landmarks.json` is flagged `approx: true`. Commons geosearch around `main-bazaar`
(1.55873, 110.34713) returned only north-bank items (Fort Margherita stairs 21 m, DUN 30 m), so the point sits near
the east end of the waterfront, not mid-street. Around `plaza-merdeka` and `carpenter-street-harmony-arch`
geosearch finds the General Post Office, Textile Museum and Chinese-style Gate within 25-115 m, which fits.
Commons has almost no geotagged street-level shophouse photos. Most useful files were found via categories
(`Category:Main Bazaar, Kuching`, `Carpenter Street, Kuching`, `India Street, Kuching`, `Old Court House, Kuching`,
`Square Tower, Kuching`, `Jalan Padungan`, `Jalan Padungan Arch`, `Cat statues, Jalan Padungan`, `Old Sarawak State Mosque`,
`Indian Mosque, Kuching`, `Kuching Waterfront`).
