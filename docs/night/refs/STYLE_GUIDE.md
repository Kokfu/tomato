# Kuching style guide (modelling rules)

Sources: our own reading of the CC photos in `img/` (see `LICENSES.md`) plus general Malaysian
building and road practice. Rows marked **(photo)** were seen in a downloaded image; **(typ)** is typical
Malaysian practice that no image here proves for Kuching; check it against a photo before calling it verified.
Colours are rough sRGB hex taken from weathered, humid-climate surfaces (never pure white, never saturated).
Weathering rule: every wall gets a mould/rain-streak darkening from the roofline down (x0.85 value at the top
edge, black-green streaks `#4a4f43` under sills and AC units).

Windows: grille patterns are plain orthogonal grids with a decorative circle or sun-burst on better houses (typ).
Roller shutters are ubiquitous; at night they are down and the five-foot way is lit by a single fluorescent tube `#f2f6f0`.

## 2. Streets (M4)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Monsoon / storm drain** (typ) | Open concrete U-drain beside the carriageway, 0.6-1.2 m wide, 0.6-1.2 m deep; **concrete slab covers** 0.6-1.0 m long at driveways and shop frontages; steel grating at junctions; water `#6f6a58` after rain, concrete `#8a8a85` with green slime at the waterline |
| **Kerb** | 150 mm concrete kerb `#9a9a95`; painted **black-and-white alternate blocks** at roundabouts, medians and junction islands (photo: `padungan_gate_cat`, `padungan_block`, `street_modern`); yellow paint = no parking (typ) |
| **Five-foot way** | 1.5 m deep arcade, floor tile `#a89c8a` or grey-red mosaic, step up 0.15 m, tiled in 300x300 grid; often blocked by stalls (photo: `mainbazaar_arcade`) |
| **Lane markings** (typ Malaysian) | Left-hand traffic. Dashed white centre line (3 m dash, 6 m gap), solid white no-overtake line or double white; edge line solid white; **yellow zig-zag/zebra bars** at crossings (photo: `signals_zebra`: yellow bars, white edges); stop line white 300 mm; lane 3.0-3.5 m |
| **Lamp posts** | Galvanised or green-painted pole 8-10 m, single arm over the lane; some twin-arm on the median (photo: `street_modern`); LED/sodium warm `#ffd9a0` at night |
| **Signals** | Black housing, red/yellow/green, pole-mounted at kerb 3 m high, pedestrian push-button box (photo: `signals_zebra`) |
| **Bus stop** | Steel-roofed shelter 4-6 m wide, blue/grey frame, bench; name board on pole; city bus livery green/yellow (photo: `bus_city`) |
| **Street trees** | **Rain tree** (Samanea saman): wide umbrella canopy 12-18 m, trunk 0.8 m, dark bark `#4d4338`, canopy `#4f6b3a`; **royal/fan palms** 8-15 m in avenues and compounds (photo: `citymosque_view`, `padungan_gate_cat` has cypress-like columnar trees); frangipani on small lots; angsana along main roads (typ) |
| **Street furniture** | Pillar boxes small red `#a23a2e`, rubbish bins green, concrete bollards, bilingual street signs on blue boards (typ) |
| **Signage (verified)** | Padungan arch is an official Chinatown gate; Carpenter St gate reads Chinese "亞答街" (photo: `carpenter_gate`); bilingual Malay/Chinese council signs exist near Kuching Central (not downloaded). **Never invent shop names**; use generic type boards. |

## 3. Atmosphere (M2)

- **Sky:** equatorial (1.5°N), high humidity: clear zenith `#5f9ed1`, horizon wash `#c9d8df` (haze band 15-25% of dome), frequent cumulus `#f4f6f6`; overcast `#a9b3b6`. Sun passes near-overhead: midday shadows are short and sharp-edged; golden hour is brief (~30 min); dusk to dark in under 40 min (~06:50 sunrise, ~18:50 sunset all year).
- **Haze / fog:** distance haze tint `#c7d3d6`, exp2 density ~0.0009-0.0015; morning river mist `#dfe6e6`. Rain mood: desaturate -30%, sky `#8f999c`, wet roads darken to `#4b4f52` with specular streaks.
- **Light:** key colour 5800 K, strong white; shade is cool blue-grey; ambient high (0.55).
- **River:** Sarawak River is silty **tea-brown** `#8a6b4a` to olive `#6f6a43`, opaque; low specular, small chop, tidal; boats: wooden *tambang* sampans (cream/white with canopy) and river taxis (photo: Commons `Kuching river taxi 20250822.jpg`, not downloaded). Bank vegetation: nipah palm, mangrove `#3f5a34`.
- **Vehicles (mix by share):** kapcai motorbikes 40% (Honda EX5/Wave, Yamaha Y15: 1.9 m long, 0.75 m wide; rider in helmet, often 2-up); Perodua Myvi/Axia/Bezza 25% (3.6-4.2 m; colours silver `#b8bcbf`, white, red `#a02a2a`, black); Proton Saga/Persona/X50 10%; Toyota Hilux/Isuzu D-Max pickups 12% (5.3 m, white/silver/black); vans/lorries 8% (4.5-7 m, white); city buses 2-3% green/yellow livery (photo: `bus_city`); taxis (red/white) negligible. Plates: Sarawak plates start with **Q** (Q + letters + 1-4 digits), black on white, front/rear.
- **Surface palette:** road asphalt `#55575a` (worn `#6a6c6f`), lane paint `#d8d8d0`, concrete pavement `#aba79f`, red-brown laterite shoulder `#9a6a4a`, grass `#5f8a3a`, wet grass `#42702e`.
