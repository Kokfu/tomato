# V7.4: street immersion (Jalan China five-foot ways and three shop interiors)

Scope: the existing Jalan China section only. No new landmarks, map data, vehicles, UI or renderer.

**Accepted scope (partial acceptance after review):** the five-foot-way collision fixes, stairs, terrain corrections and the shop interiors are in. The pedestrian prototype is **disabled by default**: the stylised figures did not fit the scene. Its route and movement code is kept for future realistic models. Committed locally; not pushed, not deployed.

## 1. Five-foot way

A collision probe (`tools/dev/walkway-probe.ts`) walked both rows before any change and found the defects below. The same probe after the change reports none of them.

| Defect found | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Old V4 five-foot-way floors still active under the section (different lot divisions) | Player stood up to 0.31 m above the visible floor for 1 to 4 m in 6 lots of the west row, with a second invisible drop mid-lot | Floors of blocks the section replaces are ignored while it is shown (`CollisionWorld.groundAt`). Also fixes the Carpenter Street section |
| Height change between lots | One step of 0.22 to 0.51 m at each party wall, no stair geometry | Concrete flights in the walk-through openings, 2 to 4 risers of 0.115 to 0.178 m, 0.30 m treads, each tread stood on |

| Party walls behind the piers had no collision | Player passed through 0.22 m and 0.25 m of visible wall beside each opening | Both stubs block; the opening itself (1.33 m wide, 2.6 m high, at least 2.1 m of headroom on the top tread) is clear |

| East row shopfront line | Player could get 0.19 m into the wall at the north end | Shopfront line blocks on both rows |

Also added: soft shade where the floor meets the shopfront and party walls, a lighter walkway ceiling (bounce light), and for Nos. 29, 27 and 25 a frame lining the shop opening with a floor track, shutter guide channels (27) and gate leaves, rails and a lock plate (29, 25).

Continuous walk, both rows, 0.35 m character: 0 blocked samples, 0 unsupported samples, largest single step 0.178 m (was 0.514 m).

## 2. Interiors (Nos. 29, 27, 25)

- **No. 27 Ann Kee (open, lit) and No. 29 Heng Kee (closed gate, dim):** the block counters are replaced by a U of glass display counters (plinth with recessed kick, padded deck, trays of rings, chains and bangles, aluminium-framed glass, light strip) round a centre aisle on the entrance axis; glazed wall cabinets on three walls; a framed panelled door, a safe, stools, counter mirrors, a clock, an altar shelf, downlights. Counter height 0.95 m, door 2.1 m, room 4.75 m x 4.15 m.
- **No. 25:** its use was checked against what the project holds. The only evidence is OpenStreetMap node 12779002965, "Yong Hu Goldsmith" (mapped April 2025), which falls mid-lot (station 18.9 m; the lot spans 16.5 to 22.0 m). The neighbouring nodes fit the photographed shops at Nos. 27 and 21, so the position is credible, but the 2015 photograph shows only a closed gate and an illegible board. One source, not photographed: the interior is therefore explicitly approximate, one plain counter and one empty cabinet with no stock, no altar and no name. The lot's inspection note says so.
- All three interiors are typical fit-outs, not photographed, and are labelled that way. They describe no current (2026) condition. They are not enterable.

## 3. Pedestrians: built, reviewed, disabled by default

A prototype was built (`src/v5/pedestrians.ts`): up to three pedestrians on fixed back-and-forth routes down the middle of the five-foot ways, walking at 1.10 to 1.35 m/s, pausing at marked stops, waiting while the player is in the way, following the stairs, validated against the collision world. The only licensed animated model at hand was the player's CC0 low-poly "Casual Character", re-coloured: the independent critique scored it 4 / 10 ("read as placeholders"), and it accounted for the whole frame-time regression (section 5). It is therefore off.

While off, nothing of it exists at run time: the module is a separate 5.7 kB chunk that is not fetched, no model is loaded, nothing is added to the scene, nothing is updated. Checked in the browser: not enabled, no instance, no scene object, chunk not requested. No pedestrian asset was ever added to the repository (the prototype reuses the player's model), and the held capture poses were removed from the saved viewpoints.

For future work with a realistic model: `?peds=on` or `__k3d.v5.setPedestrians(true)` switches the prototype on in the Jalan China section; the routes, pacing, stop and wait logic and the route validator are model-independent. Known cost to design around: each figure was 4 skinned meshes drawn in both the colour and the shadow pass with culling disabled.

**Audio:** no licensed street recording was sourced, so none was added. The existing synthesised ambience is unchanged.

Verdicts: walkway **not improved visually** from the fixed cameras (its gains are in collision and steps, which a still does not show); interiors **improved** for No. 27 and **marginal** for Nos. 29 and 25; pedestrians **marginal**.

With the pedestrians gone, what the batch delivers visually is the interiors; the walkway gains are felt when walking, not seen in a still.

## 5. Performance

Production builds of V7.3 and V7.4 served side by side, same machine, High preset, 1280 x 960, measured alternately; average frame time in ms. Run-to-run noise is about 2 ms.

Final state (pedestrians off), three alternating rounds:

| View | V7.3 | V7.4 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 along street | 25.3 / 24.2 / 25.9 | 24.5 / 24.8 / 24.8 | -1.7% |
| 02 shopfronts | 29.5 / 27.3 / 27.9 | 27.8 / 27.7 / 27.3 | -2.2% |
| 03 five-foot way | 24.8 / 23.9 / 23.9 | 24.2 / 24.5 / 25.1 | +1.7% |
| Mean | 25.9 | 25.6 | -0.9% |

All differences are inside the noise: no measurable cost. Low preset (390 x 844): 16.7 ms before and after (display-capped).

**The +11.7% at view 02, investigated.** With the pedestrians switched on, the earlier measurement was +5.6% / +11.7% / +4.5% at the three views. Removing only the pedestrians brings every view back to the V7.3 level, so the whole regression was theirs: 12 skinned meshes that are skinned and drawn every frame in both the colour pass and the 4096 px shadow pass, with frustum culling off. The interiors, glass and stairs cost nothing measurable, so no further optimisation was made.

Section geometry: 204,126 to 212,610 triangles (+4.2%); draw calls 55 to 56 (the clear-glass material); no new textures; no new dependency.

## 7. Remaining defects and limitations

1. No street life: the section has no pedestrians until a realistic, licensed, animated human model is found.
2. Interiors are lit by baked vertex colour, so there are no light pools and no spill onto the walkway; the lit shop is tidy and symmetric, with no staff. Nos. 29 and 25 are dark behind their gates.
3. Steps and kerb blocks are plain concrete; the kerb block steps can sit close to a parked car.
4. Shops are not enterable: an open shopfront stops the player at the threshold.
5. The street's fall of about 5 m comes from the terrain model and is probably steeper than reality, which is why there are so many steps.
6. The walkway probe needs the dev server (it imports source modules).
7. The Playwright set-up needs `PUBLIC_PORT=5174` to pass its console-error checks (not caused by this batch).
