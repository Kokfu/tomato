## Before (`?roads=0`) vs After (`?roads=1`) road pairs (`before-after-top-sheet.jpg`, `before-after-eye-sheet.jpg`)

- **Roundabouts (Padungan, Matang, Ong Kee Hui, Bulatan Engleham, Bulatan Abang Kipali, Demak/Salahuddin, Bulatan Abdillah, Jalan Hospital):** `?roads=0` left raw terrain with giant roadside rain trees growing inside the asphalt ring and dark overlapping road caps. `?roads=1` replaces them with black-and-white kerbed grass central islands, royal palms, splitter islands on dual-carriageway entries, double dashed give-way lines + yield triangles, and `lanes * 3.3 m` rings. On **Bulatan Engleham**, Transit Inn (`ways 233689546, 236945089`) and interior access lanes are cleanly carved out of the grass island. On **Bulatan Abang Kipali**, the 8 trunk `tunnel=yes, layer=-1` ways no longer slice across the surface island and instead enter recessed concrete portal ramps outside the ring.
- **Dual carriageways (Jalan Padungan East, Jalan Tun Abang Haji Openg):** `?roads=0` drew bare grey ground between two thin kerbs. `?roads=1` fills the median with raised grass, palms/rain trees, and `9.2 m` twin-arm galvanised street lamps, carved open at every mapped cross-street and U-turn gap and stopping clear of the Chinatown Paifang and Great Cat. (Note: the custom eye-level camera for the Openg pair in `before-after-eye-sheet.jpg` row 2 stands beside a building; tour view `12-trunk-road-openg.jpg` shows the Openg median and twin-arm lamps straight down the carriageway.)
- **Bridges & false decks (Main Bazaar, Gambier, Satok):** Splitting mixed wet/dry footways in `build.ts` removes the false `+0.3 m` concrete bridge decks and railings across `Jalan Main Bazaar` (row 10) and `Jalan Gambier` (Scenario E now completes `2,878 m` with `blocked: []`). Subdividing sparse 2-node bridge ways raises `Jambatan Tun Abdul Rahman Ya'kub` (`way/113702301`) and `< 60 m` footbridges to `>= 5.6 m` clearance, and `Jambatan Gantung Satok` (`way/1104674947`) now renders twin white H-pylons, parabolic main cables, and vertical hangers with zero river piers.
