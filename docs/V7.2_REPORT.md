# V7.2: real-asset proof of concept (Jalan China)

Question: do properly licensed, professionally authored assets give a clear realism gain over the V7.1 procedural ones?

**Result: no. The independent critic rated the change "marginal" (overall 4.4 → 4.6). The acceptance gate is not met.** The vehicle model was rejected before integration; the two photographic materials work and are cheap to download, but only the road is visible at normal viewing size.

## Outcome after the test

The user chose to keep the road material only. The plaster set, its shader and its files were removed, and the sedan was never integrated. What remains in the code: Poly Haven Asphalt 03 on the Jalan China carriageway (493,712 bytes, fetched when the section opens), with `?assets=off` as the procedural fallback. The A/B figures and screenshots below describe the test as run, with both materials on in B; the road-only state was re-captured once afterwards: 55 section draw calls, 39.5 MB section texture memory (+11.2 MB over A), 25.2 to 28.2 ms average frame time (within run-to-run noise of A), no console errors; the road matches B and the facade matches A.

## What was tested

| | A (procedural, `?assets=off`) | B (default in mode `c`) |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicles | procedural cars | procedural cars (the imported sedan was rejected, see below) |
| Road | `asphalt` shader | Poly Haven Asphalt 03: albedo, normal, roughness, 1024 px, laid at its scanned 2.05 m |
| Facade No. 27 | `plaster` shader | ambientCG Painted Plaster 017: albedo, normal, roughness, 512 px, 1.5 m per tile |

Same cameras (`tools/dev/street-views.json`), lighting, High preset, 1280x960. No other shader, lighting or geometry changes. Paint and road colours are unchanged: the photo albedo is normalised by its mean and multiplied by the existing vertex colour. Road markings, collisions and geometry are untouched. Sources, hashes and licences: `docs/ASSETS.md`.

| | A | B | Difference |

## Vehicle: rejected

`innerscene.com ... sedan-car-42109739` is genuinely CC0 (stated on the page and inside the GLB), but it is a flat-shaded low-poly sedan built from primitives: 16,300 triangles in 72 meshes, only 2,208 of them body and cab; flat colours, no textures; opaque glass with no interior; box headlights. It has the same defects the V7.1 critic named for the procedural cars, at about 16 times the triangles each. It was not integrated and is not in the repository. No substitute model was downloaded.

## Recommendation per asset

- **Sedan GLB: reject.** Done.
- **Asphalt 03: keep only if the experiment is kept at all.** It is the one visible improvement and removes a real defect (banded noise), for 494 KB.
- **Painted Plaster 017: reject for street use.** Invisible from the street; it only helps at arm's length. Not worth rolling out to other facades.

## Where the bottleneck is

Not building geometry and not lighting: the critic called massing and shadows adequate. In order:

1. **Vehicles.** They fill a third to half of every street-level frame and are the least credible objects. Free CC0 low-poly cars are the same tier as the procedural ones. What is needed is mid-poly cars (roughly 30k to 80k triangles) with real glass, an interior and textured lights, which are normally paid or CC-BY assets.
2. **Photographic fidelity as weathering, not as base texture.** Clean tiling scans of plain asphalt and plain plaster are nearly featureless. The real street reads as real because of stains, patches, streaks, worn paint and grime, which need decals or per-surface painted maps.
3. **Scene clutter.** Air-conditioner stacks, tangled cables, awnings, plants, stocked shop interiors.

Per the gate: stop further cosmetic material work. If realism is still wanted, the next spend should be one decision, not more tuning: buy or licence two or three mid-poly car models (CC-BY or commercial), since nothing else changes the score while the cars stay as they are.

## How to A/B

Mode `c` with `?assets=off` in the URL is A; without it is B. At run time: `__k3d.v5.setRealAssets(false)`.
