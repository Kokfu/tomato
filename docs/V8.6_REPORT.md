# V8.6: Low-preset shader stalls (mobile)

Branch `v5-rendering-experiment`, on top of V8.5 (`cc66118`). Not committed.

**Every number here comes from emulation, not a real phone.** The setup was Edge on an Intel UHD integrated GPU
(ANGLE D3D11), 390x844 CSS px at DPR 3, touch, CPU throttled 4x, Low preset, with a fresh browser profile for each
run. No Android phone or iPhone was available, so real-device validation is still incomplete.

## 1. Cause (instrumented, not guessed)

`tools/dev/drive-bench.ts` now records, for every shader program compiled during driving, the objects and materials
that use it and its full three.js program key. The key of each late program was diffed against the programs prepared
earlier. Three causes came out of it:

| Late program (V8.5, cold) | Stall | Differs from the prepared program in | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 x `physical/STANDARD`, the V7 Jalan China section: plain meshes, temple, food court and opera stage LOD0 | 230-780 ms each, 1.2-1.6 s per run | `shadowMapEnabled` only | **Shadow-state conflict.** On Low, the V5 pipeline turned shadows on (`V5_TIERS.low.shadowMap = 1024`) and V8.1 prepared every program with shadows. Then any resize, orientation change or adaptive-resolution step ran `applyResolution()`, which turned shadows off again (the world preset Low has `shadowMap: 0`). From then on every material needed a second, shadow-less program the first time it was drawn. |
| `dun-gold` (DUN roof) | 255-300 ms | `toneMapping` only (Neutral prepared, AgX drawn) | **Mode switch after chunk build.** The DUN chunk is built during start-up under the V4 lighting. The default V7 mode then switches tone mapping, and `setMode` prepared only the section group, not the chunks already in the scene. |
| `lambert` (Harmony Arch plaque, `modelMaterials.ts:107`) | frame 35-170 ms | (new material on every load) | **Not a draw-time stall.** The plaque material is re-created on each load of the landmark model and compiles inside the V8.1 asynchronous landmark preparation (`landmark-prepare`: programs 98 -> 99, about 15 ms synchronous) before the model joins the scene. The rest of that frame is the model's own asynchronous load. Left as is. |

The causes were **not** different material defines, late-loaded models (as a cause), incomplete texture preparation
or compile timing.

The same conflict was behind a much larger freeze that the drive benchmark never saw. On V8.5, the first look round
after Start on Low compiled **25-42 programs in one 3.9-8.5 s frame**, whenever something had called
`applyResolution()` in between. The probe unticks adaptive resolution, which does that, just as an adaptive step
or an orientation change would.

## 2. Fix (3 small source changes)

- `src/v5/controller.ts`: `V5_TIERS.low.shadowMap = 0`. Low means no shadows on both render paths. You chose
  this (the alternative was 1024 px shadows on Low). Phones were already getting no shadows after their first
  adaptive step, so this is what they actually saw, and it is the cheapest option.
- `src/main.ts`, `applyResolution`: while the V5 pipeline draws, it owns the shadow state, and a resize no longer
  changes it.
- `src/v5/controller.ts`, `setMode`: prepares the whole world scene (asynchronous `renderer.compile` through the
  existing V8.1 `pipeline.prepare`), not just the section. Chunks built before the mode switch get their new variants
  during loading instead of during play.
- `src/main.ts`, preset switch (`onQuality`): prepares the scene for the new preset and keeps the last frame on screen
  until the new variants report compiled (at most 3 s). The three call sites now share one `prepareObject` helper.

I also tried preparing chunk meshes and decorations before they join the scene (yielding frames while they compile),
then **removed it**. It fixed no measured stall (the DUN case was really the mode switch), and preparing a chunk's
decorations cost 25-157 ms in one frame.

Startup, streaming and memory: there is no blocking load step. The 13 extra programs compile asynchronously while the
game loads (85 -> 98 programs at ready). After a full drive 96 programs exist instead of 138. Chunk streaming is
unchanged.

## 4. Mobile compatibility (emulated only)

Tests run in the mobile-390 and mobile-360 projects (Chromium touch emulation): `touch-drive.spec`, which covers
steering and gas together (multitouch), brake into reverse, handbrake, enter, exit, the camera button, drag-look,
RESET, GPS by touch, release after blur, orientation change and pagehide, and portrait and landscape layouts.
`touch.spec` covers walking. The new `touch-lowprep.spec` covers the following on Low: portrait -> landscape ->
portrait while driving keeps the shadow state and compiles no program, pinch-out zooms the chase camera, and
backgrounding (`document.hidden` + `visibilitychange`) pauses the game and releases a held gas pedal, after which
Resume drives on with no new program. All pass on both sizes. **Not tested on a real Android or iOS device.**

## 5. Regression tests

The final code passes 28/28: `driving.spec` (all 10), `gps`, `landmarks` (6), `opmode` (3), the new `shader-prep`
(2), and `touch-drive`, `touch` and `touch-lowprep` on mobile-390 and mobile-360. An earlier batch also passed
`gameplay` (8) and `district` (4). The new `shader-prep.spec` test 1 was run against the V8.5 source and **fails
there** (the shadow state flips on resize), so it guards the cause. `tsc --noEmit` is clean. The full 27-minute
suite was not run.

**The intermittent desktop driving test** (`driving.spec`, "walk to the car … walk on") is timing-sensitive. It is
not a gameplay defect. An in-page frame log showed:
- Key holds through Playwright's keyboard drove n+2 to n+11 frames (fixed 1/60 s steps), and between the steps the
  car coasted 30-140 frames while awaited DOM checks ran. Its speed when braking therefore varied from 5.1 to 7.6 m/s,
  and where it stopped varied by about 20 m.
- At some stop positions (s = 58-59, 64-70 and 74-75 m) the old walk-away target, a straight 6 m line, ran into the
  V7 parked-car rows. A sweep of every stop position from s = 57 to 105 found **no trap**: wherever the way back is
  blocked, the character walks out ahead of the car. Collision and the exit spot behave correctly.

The test now holds keys for exactly n frames (key events dispatched in the page), reads the moving-car HUD 20 frames
after the W hold in one in-page step, brings the car to 8 m/s and brakes on the same frame (45 frames, still
`|vf| < 1.6`), and walks into the free lane behind the car, or ahead of it when a parked car blocks that way. No
threshold was loosened. Result: 9/10 passed. The tenth timed out waiting for the game to load at page start (90 s),
before any driving step. Before the fix: 7/8 on this run and 6/8 in V8.5.

## 6. Remaining limitations

- The Harmony Arch plaque material is re-created and recompiled on every load of that landmark model. It runs off
  the draw path, but its frame (35-170 ms throttled) still carries the model's asynchronous load. Reusing one plaque
  material per text texture would remove the recompile.
- A preset switch freezes for 1.5-2 s at the click on the throttled profile, mostly synchronous work at the moment
  of the switch (program set-up, environment map, chunk rebuild). Holding the last frame only covers the GPU part.
- The remaining >100 ms frames during driving are not shader compiles. They come from chunk builds, decorations and
  rendering at 4x CPU throttle (see `slowFrameAttribution` in the JSON).
- Without `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`, which is rare on current phones, preparation still starts compiles early,
  but a program not yet linked blocks its first draw.

## Tools added or extended

- `tools/dev/drive-bench.ts`: per late compile, the objects and materials using the program and its full key, plus
  every program key at the end. `--who-render` names the drawn object or scene behind each new program and times
  landmark preparation.
- `tools/dev/preset-probe.ts`: cold start-up and Low -> Medium -> High -> Low switches on the emulated phone.
