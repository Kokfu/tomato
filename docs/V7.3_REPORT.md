# V7.3: authentic shopfront reconstruction (Jalan China Nos. 29, 27, 25)

Scope: three adjoining west-row lots that reference photo [A] shows clearly: Heng Kee Goldsmith (29), Ann Kee Goldsmith (27) and the three-storey rose building (25). Nothing else in the section, the map, the vehicles or the renderer was changed. Not committed, not deployed.

## What changed, and what it rests on

| Lot | From photo [A] | Not in the photo (labelled as typical or approximated) |
|---|---|---|
| 29 Heng Kee | Octagonal pink plaque column (HK lozenge, 興基金鋪, "HENG KEE GOLDSMITH 29") standing clear of the eave on braced steel arms; large pink fascia board leaning out over the awning (lozenge, 29, brush characters, "KEDAI EMAS HENG KEE"); deeper eave; two condensers at sill level; roof aerial; rain streaks on the wall, pilaster and pier; closed grille | Goldsmith interior behind the grille |
| 27 Ann Kee | Paler yellow wall; small-paned casements; teal corrugated awning over the upper window; low parapet with the green terrace railing directly above it (the raised centre block is gone) and a flat roof; olive plaque column under the round diamond emblem, blue-on-yellow "ANN KEE GOLDSMITH 27"; leaning yellow board with red characters, blue Latin line and diamond; open front | Goldsmith interior (counters with trays, lit wall showcase, stools, safe, altar shelf, clock). First plaque character is still 安 read from the romanised name |
| 25 | Red fascia board moved down to the bressummer; tall blank red projecting sign under a logo panel; dentil course; darker tympanum; plants on the top balcony; grooved piers | Logo drawn as a plain blue ring (illegible in the photo); goldsmith interior behind the grille |

Typography: sign characters are drawn in a brush (Kai) face with a drop shadow, sized and placed as on the real boards. The face comes from the viewing device (`KaiTi`, `DFKai-SB`, `Kaiti TC`, ...); devices without one fall back to a serif face.

## Comparison

`docs/screenshots/v7.3/`: `compare-01-photo-before-after.png` and `compare-02-photo-before-after.png` (photo [A] crop, before, after), plus `before-*`, `after-*`, `mobile-low-*` and the stats JSON. All frames use the saved OP viewpoints in `tools/dev/street-views.json` and are captured with the HUD, V5 bar and reference panel hidden.

Still different from the photo: the first Ann Kee character; board and wall colours are cleaner and more saturated than the weathered originals; streaks are subtle; the lots' real window layout behind the columns is not visible in [A] and stays a continuous band; interiors are invented.

## Code

- `src/v5/china.ts`: the three lot specs and the new parts (`column`, `tiltBoard`, `condensers`, `goldsmith`, `hood`, `antenna`, `rustic`, `vsign`, `stains`).
- `src/v5/shophouse.ts`: opt-in lot options (`eave`, `parapetH`, `crest`, `flat`, `bare`, `panes`). Defaults are unchanged, so other lots build as before.
- `src/v5/V5Scene.ts`: `SignAtlas.paint()` for custom-drawn boards, and a skyline packer replacing the row packer. The old packer wasted whole rows under tall vertical signs and overflowed once the new boards were added; the atlas now ends at 1280 of 2048 px with nothing dropped.
