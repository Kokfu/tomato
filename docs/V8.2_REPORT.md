# V8.2 Chunk streaming hitches and safe vehicle reset

Scope: two issues only. (1) Reduce the remaining chunk-generation frame spikes while driving. (2) Make R always put
the car at a verified safe place, or say clearly that there is none. No traffic, cars, roads, landmarks, rendering
modes or materials were changed.

Status: implemented and measured; **not committed, not pushed, not deployed**.

Machine for every number below: Intel UHD Graphics (ANGLE / Direct3D 11), Edge, 1920 x 1080, medium preset,
adaptive resolution off, production builds served by `vite preview` (`tools/dev/drive-bench.ts`, same routes as V8.1).
Baseline = V8.1 (`074dd9a`), built and measured in the same session. Raw data: `docs/perf-v8.2.json`.

## 1. Root cause of the remaining chunk hitches

Per-frame attribution (new per-stage build timing in `drive-bench.ts`) on the 2 km high-speed run (scenario D):

- **Each chunk mesh was built in one call, in one frame.** The worst single builds: old-town chunk `0_0` 75-81 ms,
  of which the **buildings stage 61-72 ms**; other chunks 25-52 ms in roads (up to 34 ms), terrain and ground
  texture painting (up to 22 ms), landmark models (up to 31 ms for the waterfront chunk, at low detail too), and
  decorations (up to 28 ms). With the frame's render on top, these were the 50-120 ms frames.
- The rest of the slow frames are **not CPU build work**: Long Animation Frame data shows 40-90 ms waits before the
  frame callback starts (GPU busy with uploads and drawing on the integrated GPU), plus one-off shader programs
  (`basic`, `depth` variants, 50-125 ms) on the block loop. Neither is chunk generation; both are listed below.
- Ruled out again: collision registration (`addChunk` under 1.6 ms per slow frame in total), JSON parse (under 1.5
  ms), disposal and unloading (under 11 ms in total per run), physics and camera.

## 2. Fix (Task 1)

**Chunk builds are resumable and run against a per-frame time budget** (`src/world/ChunkManager.ts`):

- The builders became generators that yield between units of work: roads (`RoadBuilder.ts`), buildings and the
  final buffer builds (`BuildingBuilder.ts`), land-use polygons and texture mottling (`TerrainBuilder.ts`), and
  landmark models (`LandmarkManager.ts`). Their output is unchanged: same geometry, same merged meshes, same draw
  calls.
- `ChunkManager.runJobs` runs one build at a time, nearest chunk first (decorations of built chunks before new
  chunks, as before), stepping it until the frame's budget is spent: 6 ms while driving, 8 ms walking, 14 ms at
  OP-mode flying speeds, at least one step per frame. A heavy chunk now takes 2-5 frames instead of one.
- **No half-built chunk is ever drawn**: the chunk group joins the scene only when its build is complete, and a
  chunk changing detail level keeps its old mesh until the new one is swapped in (same frame). A build is
  abandoned if the chunk is unloaded, wanted at another detail level, or its settings change.
- **Collision coverage is unchanged**: it is still registered when the chunk data arrives, before any mesh.
- **The car cannot enter ground whose road is not drawn yet**: the vehicle's "loaded" test is now collision loaded
  *and* chunk mesh built (`Driving.ts`). It never triggered in any measured run (0 unbuilt frames, no 'loading'
  block), so it is a guarantee, not a behaviour change.
- **Loading time**: until the player presses Start the budget is 40 ms (`loadingBoost`, set in `main.ts`), so the
  loading screen is not slowed. Navigation to ready, 3 alternating runs each: V8.1 5.3 / 6.0 / 5.3 s, V8.2
  5.1 / 5.6 / 5.2 s. First 360-degree look round unchanged (worst 438-498 ms vs 414-476 ms, the known section
  first-draw cost).
- V8.1 shader preparation untouched.

Reading:

- **High-speed driving (D), the target:** the most build work in one frame fell from 35-81 ms to 11-20 ms in every

- **Streaming and collision:** every driven run reached its end with no building or water entry, no impacts, no

  frames, most build work in one frame 17.3 ms.
- **Frames over 200 ms:** 0 in every run of both builds.

## 4. Safe vehicle reset (Task 2)

What R did before (V8.1): try the last remembered road pose (stored only within 2.48 m of the OSM centre line), then
seven points *on the centre line*, accepting the first that passed `canOccupy`.

Defects found:

1. **Centre-line candidates only.** In Jalan China the centre line runs through the east parked row, so there was
   nothing to find unless the remembered pose happened to qualify.
2. **A body wholly inside an obstacle passed the check.** `canOccupy` uses `boxContact`, which tests outline
   *edges*; with no edge crossing the body it reports "free". A V7 parked car (about 1.8 x 4.2 m) is larger than the
   drivable car (1.73 x 3.9 m), so a centre-line candidate could be placed inside one. Shown by a unit test.
3. No clearance margin, no slope check, no drawn-road check, and no distinction between "nothing safe here" and
   "still loading".

New search (`VehicleBody.check` and `resetToRoad`, `src/vehicle/VehiclePhysics.ts`):

- **Verification of every candidate at the moment R is pressed** (`check`):
  - every probe point of the body plus a margin (0.2 m each side, 0.3 m at each end) has collision loaded and its
    chunk mesh built;
  - the centre lies within the mapped half width of a **drivable** carriageway and all four body corners satisfy the
    on-road rule (so never a pedestrian street, footway, path, steps or pier);
  - the wheels' ground: grade along the car at most 16 %, across at most 10 %, twist and centre-to-wheel step at
    most 0.2 m; no deck unless the carriageway is a road bridge (no footbridge or pier);
  - no water; no probe point inside any outline (the inside test fixes defect 2); and the margin box clear of every
    wall, pier, five-foot-way obstacle and parked car (`boxContact`).
- **Candidates, in order**: poses remembered while driving (verified when stored, kept twice a second, newest first,
  within 200 m); then places on the carriageway nearest the car, along it up to 30 m either way (re-projected on the
  centre line through bends and junctions) and **across its whole mapped width**, nearest first, facing along the
  road (with a one-way street's traffic); then remembered poses further away; then places near the newest
  remembered pose.
- **No safe place**: the car is not moved. The message is "Cannot reset yet: the road here is still loading" when
  ground or road data is still loading, else "No safe place on the road nearby: the car stays here".
- The map, the roads and the parked cars are unchanged; the search only looks for room on them.

Verified (e2e `default V7, V8.2: R puts the car at a verified safe place`, real data, default V7 rendering):

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Jalan China, car on the OSM centre line in the east parked row, nothing remembered | Lane middle (o = 0.02 m), within 0.2 m of where it was along the street, facing with the one-way traffic; then drove off with no contact |
| Near parked cars (swerved into the east row after driving the lane) | Back to the newest remembered lane pose |
| Inside a shophouse of the west row | Lane middle beside it (o = -0.27 m) |
| Jalan Carpenter, across the street with the nose in the five-foot way | On Jalan Carpenter, first candidate |
| Jalan China / Jalan Carpenter junction, diagonal | On a carriageway next to the junction |
| Unloaded ground 2.5 km away | Car not moved, "Cannot reset yet: the road here is still loading"; with a remembered pose in loaded ground, back to it |

Each success was checked independently in the page: not inside any building or parked car, no outline within
0.15-0.2 m of the body, on a drivable road, on a built chunk, speed 0. The V4 R regression (back to the last road
pose from the promenade) still passes.

## 6. Remaining limitations

- **GPU-bound frames.** Most remaining 50-100 ms frames while driving have little CPU work: the integrated GPU is busy
  (uploads of freshly built chunks and the V7 section's drawing). Spreading uploads or reducing GPU cost is a
  rendering change and was out of scope.
- **One-off program compiles** (`MeshBasicMaterial` and depth variants, 50-137 ms, once per session) remain, as in
  V8.1.
- **Indivisible steps.** One building, one road or one landmark model is still built in one go; the largest steps
  measured were 17-20 ms (a long road polyline at low detail, the waterfront landmark).
- **Reset search limits.** It looks 30 m along the road from the car (and from the newest remembered pose); a car
  stuck with every place in that range blocked gets the "no safe place" message. Road widths are the mapped
  (mostly inferred) ones; the search trusts them only together with the obstacle and outline checks.
- **Vehicle loading guard.** Requiring the chunk mesh before the car enters ground means that if a mesh is ever
  invalidated under a moving car (quality change), the car pauses with "Loading the next area…" until it is rebuilt.
- These are measurements on one machine over two benchmark pairs; they do not prove a spike cannot occur elsewhere.
